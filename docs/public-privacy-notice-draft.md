# CIRS Public Privacy Notice Draft

This draft is a public-facing privacy notice for a CIRS deployment operated by an LGU.

It is written in plain language so it can be adapted for a website, dashboard onboarding flow, or citizen-facing mobile surface.

Replace all bracketed placeholders before publication.

## 1. Who Is Responsible For Your Data

`Tuguegarao City pilot deployment sample` operates the CIRS platform and acts as the personal information controller for personal data processed through the service.

For privacy questions or requests, you may contact:

- Office or unit: `Office of the Data Protection Officer - sample`
- Data Protection Officer: `DPO Office - sample contact`
- Email: `dpo@tuguegarao.example.gov`
- Address: `Tuguegarao City Hall, Tuguegarao City, Cagayan - sample address`

## 2. What CIRS Is For

CIRS is used to receive, manage, route, and monitor incident reports and related public-safety or service-response activities.

Depending on the deployment, it may also support:

1. citizen account registration and login
2. incident status follow-up
3. responder and dispatcher coordination
4. audit, security, and compliance review
5. privacy request and incident handling

## 3. What Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with the service, we may collect:

1. your name, email address, mobile number, or account details
2. your incident report details, including location, description, category, status, and related communications
3. uploaded files, photos, videos, or other supporting evidence you choose to provide
4. account and security data such as login activity, device or browser details, session data, and password-reset records
5. privacy request or complaint details when you exercise your rights or raise a concern

We aim to collect only the data that is necessary for the specific service or legal purpose.

## 4. Why We Process Your Data

We process personal data to:

1. create and secure accounts when you request access to the service
2. receive, validate, route, and manage incident reports
3. support lawful public-service or emergency-response functions of the LGU
4. protect the platform, prevent abuse, and investigate misuse
5. respond to privacy requests, complaints, or incidents
6. maintain audit, accountability, and records-management controls

## 5. Our Main Lawful Bases

Depending on the activity, we may rely on one or more of these lawful bases:

1. performance of a public authority function or legitimate government service
2. fulfillment of a service you requested, such as account creation or profile linking
3. legal obligation, especially for records, rights handling, and incident documentation
4. legitimate interests in securing the platform and preventing misuse
5. consent, but only for limited cases where the processing is optional or where transparency acknowledgement is appropriate

We do not rely on consent for core security, records, or incident-response functions when those functions are required to operate the service lawfully and safely.

## 6. Who May Receive Your Data

Your data may be accessed only by authorized personnel and approved service providers on a need-to-know basis, including:

1. LGU staff or responders responsible for incident handling and coordination
2. authorized administrators, auditors, or compliance reviewers
3. approved technical providers for hosting, database, authentication, storage, or secure notifications, where applicable

Public-facing case lookup or status features are designed to show only limited and privacy-safe information.

## 7. Cross-Border Or External Processing

Some technical service providers used to run the platform may be outside the Philippines or may use infrastructure outside the Philippines.

When this applies, the operating authority should document the transfer basis, review the provider's safeguards, and maintain the required transfer and processor records.

If you want more information about the current processor or residency posture of the deployment, contact the DPO.

## 8. How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the service, accountability, records-management, legal, and security purposes that apply to the specific record type.

Different types of records may have different retention periods.

For example:

1. incident and audit records may be kept longer for accountability and investigation purposes
2. session and password-reset records may be kept for shorter operational security periods
3. privacy request and privacy incident records may be kept as long as needed for legal and governance review

Retention and disposal are governed by the operating authority's approved retention schedule.

## 9. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and operational limitations, you may have the right to:

1. request access to your personal data
2. request correction of inaccurate data
3. request deletion, blocking, restriction, or objection in appropriate cases
4. file a complaint or raise a privacy concern

To exercise your rights, contact the DPO or the office identified above.

## 10. How We Protect Data

We use organizational, technical, and administrative measures appropriate to the service, including:

1. role-based and scoped access controls
2. authentication and session-security controls
3. audit logging and security monitoring
4. restricted workflow access for sensitive records
5. breach-response and privacy-incident documentation procedures

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to maintain safeguards proportionate to the risks of the data involved.

## 11. Updates To This Notice

This notice may be updated from time to time when the service, legal position, or data practices change.

When material changes are made, the operating authority should update the published notice and, where appropriate, the onboarding notice shown to users.
