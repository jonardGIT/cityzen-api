Who Is Responsible For Your Data

Tuguegarao City pilot deployment sample operates the CIRS platform and acts as the personal information controller for personal data processed through the service.

Processing Posture Snapshot

The internal system description tracks a few baseline questions for governance and registration review. This summary mirrors that posture in public-facing language.

System and role

CIRS is the incident reporting and coordination system for the pilot deployment, and the operating LGU acts as the personal information controller for the live service.

Automated decisions

The current pilot baseline does not describe formal automated decision-making or profiling in production use.

Cross-border posture

Some external providers may operate outside the Philippines, so residency and transfer documentation must match the actual deployment.

If the same platform is later used in a separate processor role for another organization, that use should be documented and registered separately from the LGU's controller use.

Service Surfaces In Scope

The current CIRS deployment model is easier to understand when it is read as three connected surfaces: a citizen-facing service surface, an operational staff surface, and the API processing core behind both.

Surface What it does Main data involved Controller or processor posture
MyTuguegarao citizen surface Citizen-facing registration, profile linking, incident submission, and status-follow-up experience Account details, incident details, location, attachments, and related account metadata The operating LGU is normally the personal information controller for this use. If the same app is later run by a provider on behalf of another controller, that use should be documented separately.
CIRS Dashboard operational surface Browser-based staff, responder, dispatcher, admin, auditor, and reviewer interface Staff account and session data, incident records, assignments, audit views, and governance records The operating LGU is normally the personal information controller for this operational use, while external hosting or support vendors act as processors where applicable.
CIRS API processing core Backend validation, storage, authentication, audit logging, and privacy-governance workflows Operational records, auth metadata, audit data, privacy-request records, and privacy-incident records The operating LGU is the controller for live service operation, while hosting, database, auth, storage, monitoring, and messaging vendors act as processors where applicable.

The current pilot baseline does not document formal automated decision-making in any of these three surfaces. Cross-border processing depends on the actual providers used for hosting, storage, monitoring, notifications, and related infrastructure.

What CIRS Is For

CIRS is used to receive, manage, route, and monitor incident reports and related public-safety or service-response activities.

  • Citizen account registration and login
  • Incident status follow-up
  • Responder and dispatcher coordination
  • Audit, security, and compliance review
  • Privacy request and incident handling

What Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with the service, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, mobile number, or account details
  • Your incident report details, including location, description, category, status, and related communications
  • Uploaded files, photos, videos, or other supporting evidence
  • Account and security data such as login activity, device or browser details, session data, and password-reset records
  • Privacy request or complaint details when you exercise your rights

We aim to collect only the data that is necessary for the specific service or legal purpose.

Why We Process Your Data

  • Create and secure accounts when you request access to the service
  • Receive, validate, route, and manage incident reports
  • Support lawful public-service or emergency-response functions
  • Protect the platform, prevent abuse, and investigate misuse
  • Respond to privacy requests, complaints, or incidents
  • Maintain audit, accountability, and records-management controls

Our Main Lawful Bases

Public authority function Legitimate government service Service requested by the user Legal obligation Legitimate interests in security Consent for limited optional cases

We do not rely on consent for core security, records, or incident-response functions when those functions are required to operate the service lawfully and safely.

Who May Receive Your Data

  • LGU staff or responders responsible for incident handling
  • Authorized administrators, auditors, or compliance reviewers
  • Approved technical providers for hosting, database, authentication, storage, or secure notifications, where applicable

Public-facing case lookup or status features are designed to show only limited and privacy-safe information.

Cross-Border Or External Processing

Some technical service providers used to run the platform may be outside the Philippines or may use infrastructure outside the Philippines.

When this applies, the operating authority should document the transfer basis, review the provider's safeguards, and maintain the required transfer and processor records. If you want more information about the current processor or residency posture of the deployment, contact the DPO.

Processor category Typical data involved Sample residency posture Review expectation

How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the service, accountability, records-management, legal, and security purposes that apply to the specific record type.

Longer retention

Incident and audit records may be kept longer for accountability and investigation purposes.

Shorter retention

Session and password-reset records may be kept for shorter operational security periods.

Governance records

Privacy request and privacy incident records may be kept as long as needed for legal and governance review.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and operational limitations, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion, blocking, restriction, or objection in appropriate cases
  • File a complaint or raise a privacy concern

To exercise your rights, contact the DPO or visit the dedicated Rights & Incidents page.

How We Protect Data

  • Role-based and scoped access controls
  • Authentication and session-security controls
  • Audit logging and security monitoring
  • Restricted workflow access for sensitive records
  • Breach-response and privacy-incident documentation procedures

No system can guarantee absolute security, but we aim to maintain safeguards proportionate to the risks of the data involved.

Updates To This Notice

This notice may be updated from time to time when the service, legal position, or data practices change. When material changes are made, the operating authority should update the published notice and, where appropriate, the onboarding notice shown to users.
