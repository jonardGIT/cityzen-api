Rights Requests and Privacy Incident Guidance

This page explains how to raise privacy concerns, request access or correction, and what should happen when a privacy incident is discovered.

Who this helps Citizens, staff, and reviewers

The page is written so external requesters and internal staff can follow the same core rights and escalation path without ambiguity.

Main rule Escalate before improvising

Rights decisions and incident handling should go through the documented DPO and review workflow, not side-channel messages or ad hoc approvals.

Documentation standard Record the full decision trail

Requests and incidents both need documented identity checks, review notes, decisions, timing, and corrective actions.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and operational limitations, people may have the right to request access, correction, deletion-related review, restriction, objection, or raise a privacy concern.

Access Correction Restriction Objection Complaint

How To Raise A Rights Request

  1. Contact the DPO or designated privacy office using the published contact details.
  2. State what you are requesting as clearly as possible.
  3. Provide enough information for the operating authority to identify the record or account involved.
  4. Wait for formal review through the documented rights-handling workflow.

Staff should not improvise privacy decisions in chat, email, or verbal side conversations.

Request Matrix

Request type Typical example Handling owner What should be recorded
Access request A person asks what data the system holds about them DPO or authorized reviewer Identity check, scope of request, response decision, release date
Correction request A person reports inaccurate contact or case-linked data DPO with operational owner support Disputed field, supporting evidence, decision, correction date
Restriction or objection A person questions continued processing in a specific case DPO and system owner Lawful basis review, operational impact, final decision
Privacy complaint A person says their data was disclosed or misused DPO with security owner where needed Complaint facts, related systems, review notes, escalation outcome

What Counts As A Privacy Incident

  • Personal data is viewed by an unauthorized person
  • Personal data is sent to the wrong recipient or external party
  • A device, account, token, or system with access is compromised
  • Incident data is exported, copied, or disclosed outside approved workflows
  • A vendor reports exposure, compromise, or loss involving relevant data

Immediate Actions

Step 1 Contain the issue if possible without destroying evidence
Step 2 Notify the DPO and system owner immediately
Step 3 Create or update the privacy incident record
Step 4 Preserve logs, screenshots, and relevant audit evidence

First Assessment Questions

  • What data was involved?
  • How many people may be affected?
  • Was sensitive or identity-linked data involved?
  • Is the exposure ongoing?
  • Did a processor or vendor contribute to the incident?
  • Could affected individuals face harm if the exposure is confirmed?

Response Workflow

Detect

Acknowledge the incident and stop further unnecessary exposure.

Contain

Limit the immediate risk without losing evidence.

Assess

Evaluate severity, likely impact, and notification obligations.

Document

Record timing, decisions, corrective actions, and lessons learned.

Minimum Incident Record Fields

Field Why it matters
Incident reference and discovery time Anchors the event in the official response timeline
Reporter and affected system Shows who detected the issue and which workflow was involved
Data categories and estimated affected population Supports severity assessment and notification decisions
Containment steps Shows how the immediate risk was reduced without destroying evidence
Notification decision and timing Preserves regulator and affected-person decision rationale
Corrective actions and closure review Shows the root cause was addressed and not only documented

Contact And Follow-Up

For rights requests or privacy concerns, contact the Office of the Data Protection Officer at dpo@tuguegarao.example.gov.ph.

If you are a staff member and you discover a possible privacy incident, do not wait for certainty before escalating. Report immediately and preserve evidence.
