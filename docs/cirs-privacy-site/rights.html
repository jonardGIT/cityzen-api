Rights Requests and Privacy Incident Guidance

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law and operational limitations, people may have the right to request access, correction, deletion-related review, restriction, objection, or raise a privacy concern.

Access Correction Restriction Objection Complaint

How To Raise A Rights Request

  1. Contact the DPO or designated privacy office using the published contact details.
  2. State what you are requesting as clearly as possible.
  3. Provide enough information for the operating authority to identify the record or account involved.
  4. Wait for formal review through the documented rights-handling workflow.

Staff should not improvise privacy decisions in chat, email, or verbal side conversations.

What Counts As A Privacy Incident

  • Personal data is viewed by an unauthorized person
  • Personal data is sent to the wrong recipient or external party
  • A device, account, token, or system with access is compromised
  • Incident data is exported, copied, or disclosed outside approved workflows
  • A vendor reports exposure, compromise, or loss involving relevant data

Immediate Actions

Step 1 Contain the issue if possible without destroying evidence
Step 2 Notify the DPO and system owner immediately
Step 3 Create or update the privacy incident record
Step 4 Preserve logs, screenshots, and relevant audit evidence

First Assessment Questions

  • What data was involved?
  • How many people may be affected?
  • Was sensitive or identity-linked data involved?
  • Is the exposure ongoing?
  • Did a processor or vendor contribute to the incident?
  • Could affected individuals face harm if the exposure is confirmed?

Response Workflow

Detect

Acknowledge the incident and stop further unnecessary exposure.

Contain

Limit the immediate risk without losing evidence.

Assess

Evaluate severity, likely impact, and notification obligations.

Document

Record timing, decisions, corrective actions, and lessons learned.

Contact And Follow-Up

For rights requests or privacy concerns, contact the Office of the Data Protection Officer at dpo@tuguegarao.example.gov.ph.

If you are a staff member and you discover a possible privacy incident, do not wait for certainty before escalating. Report immediately and preserve evidence.
