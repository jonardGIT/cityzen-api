Audience view

One public microsite, different readers.

The same pages need to work for citizens, staff, and compliance reviewers. This section clarifies what each group is expected to get from the site without forcing them to parse the full internal document set.

Citizens and users

Use the privacy notice and rights page to understand data handling, retention posture, complaint routes, and DPO contact details.

LGU staff

Use the training page as a readable operational reference for handling rules, rights routing, and incident escalation expectations.

Reviewers and partners

Use the tables as the public-facing layer above the fuller lawful basis, retention, processor, and incident playbook documents in the repo.

Leadership and oversight

Use the site to verify that the public explanation stays aligned with the current privacy governance posture and escalation model.
