# Draft Privacy Governance Policy For CIRS Deployment

This document is a formal policy-style draft for LGU review and adoption.

It is intended to sit above the operational templates and playbooks in this repository and state the governance rules for a CIRS deployment in formal terms.

Replace all bracketed placeholders before adoption.

## 1. Policy Statement

`Tuguegarao City pilot deployment sample` adopts this Privacy Governance Policy for the operation of the CIRS platform to ensure that personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, proportionately, securely, and accountably.

This policy applies to all personnel, operators, contractors, and technical administrators who access, manage, support, or review data processed by the platform.

## 2. Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

1. define accountability for privacy governance in the deployment of CIRS
2. ensure compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012, its implementing rules, and related National Privacy Commission guidance
3. establish minimum rules for access control, records retention, vendor oversight, incident response, and data-subject rights handling
4. require documented and reviewable governance practices for the platform

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

1. all personal data processed through CIRS
2. all users and personnel granted access to the platform
3. all records generated by incident handling, user administration, security operations, privacy requests, and privacy incidents
4. all vendors and technical providers used to operate the platform

## 4. Governance Principles

The operating authority shall ensure that:

1. personal data is processed only for legitimate, specific, and documented purposes
2. access to personal data is limited to the minimum necessary level
3. public-facing outputs remain privacy-safe and proportionate
4. retention and disposal are governed by approved schedules and legal-hold rules
5. processors and subprocessors are documented, reviewed, and governed by appropriate contractual safeguards
6. privacy incidents are escalated, documented, and reviewed without concealment or delay
7. data-subject rights are handled through documented workflows and not by ad hoc responses

## 5. Accountability Roles

The operating authority shall designate at minimum:

1. a Data Protection Officer or equivalent accountable privacy lead
2. a system owner accountable for the deployment and governance execution
3. a security or infrastructure owner accountable for technical safeguards and evidence preservation
4. an operations lead accountable for role assignment and jurisdiction-bound access
5. a records-management owner accountable for retention, disposal, and legal-hold coordination

## 6. Required Governance Artifacts

The following documents shall be maintained and reviewed:

1. lawful basis register
2. public-facing privacy notice
3. record of processing activities
4. processor register
5. retention schedule
6. access review records
7. privacy incident playbook and incident records
8. training materials and attendance evidence

## 7. Access Control Policy

Access to CIRS shall be role-based, scope-limited, and reviewable.

The operating authority shall:

1. grant access only upon documented approval
2. limit access to the user's current operational role and jurisdiction
3. review privileged access at least monthly and broader operational access at least quarterly
4. revoke or downgrade access immediately when no longer required
5. require stronger safeguards such as MFA where the deployment policy requires it

## 8. Retention And Disposal Policy

The operating authority shall adopt a written retention schedule covering incident records, audit logs, security records, privacy requests, privacy incidents, and related data classes.

Records shall not be disposed of when a legal hold, complaint, investigation, or regulatory review applies.

## 9. Vendor And Transfer Governance Policy

All processors, subprocessors, and externally hosted services that may access personal data shall be documented and reviewed before use.

Cross-border or hybrid processing shall not proceed without documented review of residency posture, contractual safeguards, and transfer references where required.

## 10. Privacy Incident Policy

Any privacy incident or suspected unauthorized disclosure shall be promptly escalated, documented, contained, assessed, and reviewed.

Personnel shall not conceal, minimize, or resolve a privacy incident outside the approved workflow.

## 11. Training Policy

Personnel with access to CIRS shall receive privacy and security training before live access is granted and at least annually thereafter.

Additional training shall be required after major workflow changes or significant privacy or security incidents.

## 12. Review And Approval

This policy shall be reviewed at least annually and whenever there is a material change in data processing, deployment architecture, public exposure, or legal requirements.
