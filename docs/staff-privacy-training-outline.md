# Staff Privacy Training Outline

Use this outline to run privacy and security awareness training for staff using or administering the CIRS platform.

The goal is to make privacy obligations operational for LGU users, not only legal or technical.

## 2. Learning Objectives

By the end of the training, attendees should be able to:

1. Explain why CIRS processes personal data and what types of data are most sensitive.
2. Follow minimum-necessary access and handling rules.
3. Recognize a data-subject request and route it correctly.
4. Recognize a privacy incident and escalate it immediately.
5. Avoid unsafe sharing, downloading, exporting, or discussing incident data.

## 3. Core Modules

### Module A: Privacy Basics

1. Data Privacy Act of 2012 basics
2. Transparency, necessity, and proportionality
3. Roles of the LGU, staff, and DPO

### Module B: CIRS Data Handling

1. What data the platform collects
2. Why incident narratives, locations, attachments, and identities are sensitive
3. What is allowed in the system and what must not be copied outside it

### Module C: Access And Account Discipline

1. Role-based access and jurisdiction scope
2. MFA and password hygiene
3. Shared-account prohibition
4. Offboarding and role-change expectations

### Module D: Rights Requests

1. What a data-subject request looks like
2. Where to route it
3. Why staff should not improvise responses outside the workflow

### Module E: Privacy Incidents

1. What counts as a privacy incident
2. Immediate escalation expectations
3. Evidence preservation and no-cover-up rule

### Module F: Safe Daily Practice

1. Screen privacy
2. Device and browser hygiene
3. Export discipline
4. Messaging and email caution
5. Phishing and social engineering awareness

## 4. Role-Specific Add-Ons

### Responders And Dispatchers

1. Handling sensitive field information during active response
2. Avoiding unnecessary disclosure over radio, chat, or screenshots
3. Using only the assigned jurisdiction scope

### Admins And Supervisors

1. Account issuance and revocation
2. Rights-request and privacy-incident workflow oversight
3. Audit and review expectations

### Technical Operators

1. Secret handling
2. Production environment safeguards
3. Vendor and residency change review
4. Backup, restore, and log protection

## 5. Suggested Session Format

1. 10 minutes: privacy and platform overview
2. 15 minutes: platform-specific handling rules
3. 10 minutes: rights requests and privacy incidents
4. 10 minutes: role-specific expectations
5. 5 minutes: acknowledgement or short quiz

## 6. Evidence Of Completion

Keep these records:

1. Attendance list
2. Material version used
3. Delivery date
4. Quiz or acknowledgement result
5. Follow-up actions if gaps were identified

## 7. Refresher Triggers

Run refresher training:

1. At least annually
2. Before live access is granted
3. After a major workflow or policy change
4. After a significant privacy or security incident
